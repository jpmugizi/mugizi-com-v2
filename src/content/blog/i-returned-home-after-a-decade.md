In mid-July 2017, I made the journey back to Kigali, Rwanda after a decade abroad. This is the story of that return.

## The Journey

I traveled through Ethiopian Airlines, navigating the crowded conditions at Addis-Ababa's Bole International Airport before the brief connecting flight to Kigali. The anticipation was building with each leg of the journey.

## A Memory from Seven Years Prior

My last departure from Rwanda was seven years before this return. I left my family - including my father - with hasty goodbyes at the airport.

I still remember the look on his face. It is as if he was telling me "good luck son and make me proud" but I could see his worry and sadness. That image has stayed with me all these years.

## Landing in Kigali

Upon landing in Kigali, overwhelming emotions washed over me. The airport felt manageable and welcoming - a stark contrast to the chaos of Addis-Ababa.

But it was reuniting with my mother that proved most moving. Modern technology had enabled us to stay connected across the distance - video calls, messages, photos shared instantly. Yet nothing compared to our physical embrace after such prolonged separation.

## Reflection

This reunion came seven years too late in some ways. My father passed during our time apart, and I carry that with me. But I committed to maximizing the moment, to being fully present with the family I still have.

There's something profound about returning to the place where you grew up after so many years away. You see it with fresh eyes while also being flooded with memories. The city had changed, I had changed, but the feeling of home remained.
