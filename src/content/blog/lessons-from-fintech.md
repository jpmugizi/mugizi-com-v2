Working at Chipper Cash gave me a unique perspective on building financial systems for emerging markets. The challenges are different from what you'd encounter at a US-based company.

## Reliability is Non-Negotiable

When you're handling people's money, downtime isn't just an inconvenience. It's a breach of trust. Every minute of downtime means someone can't pay for groceries, can't send money to family, can't run their business.

This changes how you think about everything from deployment strategies to incident response.

## Infrastructure Constraints Shape Everything

Building for markets with unreliable internet and expensive data plans forces you to think differently about architecture. Every byte counts. Every request needs to handle failure gracefully.

## The Human Side Matters Most

The technical challenges are interesting, but the real reward is seeing the impact. Watching someone use your product to send money home to their family puts everything into perspective.

Technology is just a means to an end.
