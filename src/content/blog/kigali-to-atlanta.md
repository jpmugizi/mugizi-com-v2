I grew up in Kigali, Rwanda. The journey from there to working in tech in Atlanta has been anything but linear.

## The Early Days

My first exposure to computers was at an internet cafe. The machines were slow, the connection was unreliable, but I was hooked. There was something magical about being able to access information from anywhere in the world.

## Finding My Path

I didn't have a clear roadmap to follow. There weren't many software engineers in my immediate circle to learn from. Everything was figured out through trial and error, online resources, and a lot of persistence.

## What I've Learned

The biggest lesson has been that the path doesn't need to be perfect. It just needs to keep moving forward. Every job, every project, every failure has taught me something valuable.

The tech industry can feel exclusive sometimes, but there's always room for people willing to learn and work hard. Your background is an asset, not a limitation.
